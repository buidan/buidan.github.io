Neon Overdrive: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Neon Overdrive ("the game") is made by Bui Dinh Dan. In short: no accounts, no ads, no analytics. Local matches stay on your device; online play sends gameplay data to our game server only while you play.

Local matches

Your callsign, trail colour, settings, level and match history are stored only on your device. Local matches against AI pilots don't send anything over the internet.

Online room

When you choose ONLINE ROOM, the game connects over an encrypted connection (TLS) to our game server at play.swarmzone.click. The server receives:

  • Your callsign, trail colour and level, shown to other players in the room.
  • Your controls and in-game state (steering, boost, position), used only to run the match. They're held in memory and discarded when you leave. Match results are sent back to you and stored only on your device.
  • Your IP address, which is part of any internet connection. It may appear briefly in server logs for security and troubleshooting.

There's no chat. We don't collect your real name, email, location, contacts, device identifiers or advertising ID.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, no tracking, no analytics SDKs, no accounts.
  • No selling or sharing of your data with third parties.

Children

The game isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game removes everything stored on your device. CONFIG → RESET PILOT DATA clears it without uninstalling. The server keeps nothing after you leave a room.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
