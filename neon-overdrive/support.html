Neon Overdrive: Support

Need help or found a bug? Email buidan.infotech@gmail.com. Please include your device model and, for online issues, your callsign.

Controls (touch)

  • STEER: drag the stick left / right (or switch to gyro tilt in CONFIG).
  • 90° SNAP: tap for an instant sharp turn; hold to drift.
  • BOOST: hold for overdrive speed (burns energy when the meter is empty).
  • JUMP: tap or swipe up to clear a ribbon.
  • POWER: use your power-up (Phase Shift, EMP, Wall Extension, Magneto Line).

Common questions

How do I win? Collect energy to grow your ribbon, then cut in front of rivals so they crash into it, or cross your own ribbon to seal a loop around them. Follow the arrow ahead of your cycle to find energy.

Can I play without internet? Yes. LOCAL MATCH needs no connection. If the online room can't connect, the lobby offers a local match.

Do I need an account? No. Set your callsign in the HANGAR.

"Update the game" when joining online? Your app and the server versions differ. Update from the App Store.
