Loot & Slime Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Loot & Slime (the "game") uses, why, and the choices you have. The game is made by Bui Dinh Dan ("we"). If you have any question, write to buidan.infotech@gmail.com.

The short version

  • You don't need an account, and we never ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • Your progress is saved only on your device.
  • The game shows optional rewarded ads (you choose to watch them) and, if you agree, sends anonymous gameplay statistics and crash reports so we can improve it.
  • When the game first starts, it asks for your consent where the law requires it. You can change your choice any time in Settings.

Information stored on your device

Your progress (floors cleared, coins, gems, gear, skins, chosen look, the name you give your slime, sound and vibration settings and your privacy choice) is saved in a file on your device. It is not sent to us. Deleting the game deletes it.

Information collected by services we use

The game uses these Google services. They receive information from your device only as described below.

ServiceWhat it receivesWhy
Google AdMob Device and advertising identifiers, IP address (used to estimate your country or region), device and app information, and how you interact with ads. To show rewarded ads, measure them and prevent fraud. Ads are personalized only if you allow it.
Google Analytics for Firebase Gameplay events such as starting, winning or losing a floor, merging or upgrading gear, using an ability, equipping a look, and watching an ad. Each event carries only numbers or names from the game (for example a floor number). Also a random app-instance identifier and basic device information (model, system version, language, country). To understand which parts of the game are too easy or too hard, and to improve it.
Firebase Crashlytics Crash reports: the device model and system version, the state of the app when it crashed, and short notes about recent game steps. Game script errors are reported with only the script file name and line number. To find and fix crashes and errors.

Analytics and crash reporting start only after you agree (or where no consent is required). Nothing is collected or stored for them before that. Google processes this information under its own terms: see Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Your choices

  • Consent: if you are in a region where consent is required (such as the EEA, the UK or Switzerland), the game asks you when it first starts. Where Google requires it, Settings shows Privacy options so you can change your choice at any time.
  • Ad tracking: on iPhone and iPad, the game asks for permission before using your device's advertising identifier, and you can change this in iOS Settings. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.
  • Ads are optional: ads play only when you tap to watch one for a reward.
  • Deleting data: uninstalling the game removes everything saved on your device. To ask us to delete analytics or crash data linked to your app installation, email us; please include the date you played, as we cannot otherwise identify you.

How long information is kept

Analytics data is kept for the period set in our Google Analytics settings, at most 14 months. Crash reports are kept for 90 days.

Sharing

We do not sell your information. We do not share it with anyone other than the Google services listed above, which act for us, and AdMob, which may work with its advertising partners to fill ads under Google's policies and your consent choices.

Children

The game is meant for players aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the game and information was collected, contact us and we will help remove it.

Security

Information sent to Google's services travels over encrypted connections.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
