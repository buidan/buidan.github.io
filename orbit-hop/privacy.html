OrbitHop Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 1, 2026

This policy explains how the mobile game OrbitHop ("the app"), developed by Dan Bui Dinh ("we", "us"), handles information on Android and iOS.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, or other personal information.

Your game progress (such as scores, in-game currency, and settings) is stored only on your device. It is not sent to us, and it is deleted if you uninstall the app.

Advertising

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may collect and process information from your device, including:

  • Device advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID or Apple IDFA)
  • IP address and approximate (coarse) location derived from it
  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, and app version
  • Ad interactions, such as ad views and taps

This information is collected by Google under its own privacy policy. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): if you are in these regions, the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice by reinstalling the app or through your device's ad settings.
  • iOS: the app asks for permission to track through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", your advertising identifier is not shared and you will see non-personalized ads. You can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on your device).

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will help remove it.

Data security and retention

We do not store any personal information on our own servers. Data collected by Google AdMob is retained according to Google's policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
