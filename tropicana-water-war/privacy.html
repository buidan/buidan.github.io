Tropicana Water War
Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Tropicana Water War ("the game") is made by Bui Dinh Dan ("we"). This policy explains what information the game uses and why.

What the game itself collects

Nothing. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat and no analytics of its own. Matches are played on your device against computer-controlled opponents. Your progress (level, coins, pearls, season pass, unlocked masks and skins, daily challenge and settings) is stored only on your device and is never sent to us. Deleting the game deletes it.

Reminders (notifications)

If you agree, the game schedules reminders on your device, for example when a new Daily Splash challenge starts or your free Island Gift is ready. They are created on your device; no server or push service is involved, and no information is sent to us. You can turn them off at any time in your phone's settings.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob:

  • optional ads you choose to watch, to get back into a match (Second Wind), to double the coins you earned, or for free pearls
  • an occasional full-screen ad between matches

To show and measure ads, Google may collect and use information from your device, such as:

  • device and advertising identifiers (on iOS, the IDFA, only if you allow tracking),
  • approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • information about how you interact with ads,
  • diagnostic data (for example, crashes or ad performance).

How Google uses this information is described in Google's policies: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): before any ad loads, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy Options.
  • Tracking on iOS: the game asks for permission before using your device's advertising identifier. If you decline, you still get ads, just not personalized ones. You can change this anytime in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Ads are never required to play. Declining consent, tracking or reminders doesn't block any part of the game.

Purchases

The game has no in-app purchases. Coins and pearls are earned by playing and have no real-money value.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. Ads are configured as not child-directed and limited to content suitable for teens.
