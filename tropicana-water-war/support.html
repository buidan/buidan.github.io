Tropicana Water War

A tropical water-park battle royale for iPhone, iPad and Android. Grab a blaster, wear a Tiki mask and be the last splasher standing.

Support

Found a bug or have an idea? Email buidan.infotech@gmail.com and include your device model and what happened.

Common questions

Do I need an account or internet? No. Matches are played on your device against bots; ads need a connection but are optional.

Can I play battles in portrait? Yes: Settings → Landscape Battles → Off.

How do I turn off reminders? In your phone's Settings app, under Notifications → Tropicana Water War.

Is my progress backed up? Progress is stored on your device. Deleting the game deletes it.
