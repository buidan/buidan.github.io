Glacier Gliders privacy policy

Glacier Gliders is a game by Daian. We collect as little as possible.

On your device

Your glider name, progress, purchases and settings are saved on your device. They never leave it unless you play online.

Online play

When you turn on online play, the game connects to our match server and sends your glider name, cosmetic look, level and in-match controls so other players can see you. The server does not store them after the match, and no account is needed.

Purchases and leaderboards

In-app purchases go through the App Store or Google Play; we never see your payment details. If you sign in to Game Center or Google Play Games, your score and achievements are shared with that service under its own privacy policy.

No ads, no tracking

Glacier Gliders shows no ads, uses no analytics or advertising SDKs and does not track you across apps.
