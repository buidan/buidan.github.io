Grid Breaker

DASH. CHAIN. OVERDRIVE.

You are a node of light on a neon grid. Steer with one thumb, dash with the other, and slice through geometric enemies marching in strict formations. A dash that hits recharges instantly, so a good run never stops moving.

  • Break the gold Cores: every linked enemy nearby explodes in a chain reaction.
  • Chain for multipliers up to x10, then fill the gold ring for Overdrive.
  • Endless waves with splitters, laser links, pulsers and shielded Cores.
  • One-minute training, two-thumb or one-thumb controls, colorblind outlines.
  • Plays offline, no account needed.
