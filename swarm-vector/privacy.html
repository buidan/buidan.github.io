Swarm Vector: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Swarm Vector ("the game") is made by Bui Dinh Dan. This policy explains what data the game handles. In short: no accounts and no analytics of our own; the game shows ads through Google AdMob, and online play sends gameplay data to our server.

Offline campaign

Your progress, settings, callsign and unlocks are stored only on your device. The offline campaign doesn't send gameplay data anywhere (ads may still load if you're connected; see below).

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows optional rewarded ads and occasional full-screen ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier, approximate location from your IP address, ad interactions and diagnostics, to show and measure ads. In the EU/UK you're asked for consent first (Google's consent form; change it any time under CONFIG → PRIVACY OPTIONS). On iOS, ads are personalised only if you allow tracking. See how Google uses data from apps.

Online arena

When you choose ONLINE ARENA, the game connects over an encrypted connection (TLS) to our game server at play.swarmzone.click. The server receives:

  • Your callsign and pilot portrait number, shown to other players in the room.
  • Your controls and in-game state (steering, boost, formation), used only to run the match. They're held in memory and discarded when you leave.
  • Your best online run (callsign, score, peak swarm size, trails sliced, sector reached, date), stored on the public global leaderboard.
  • Your IP address, which is part of any internet connection. It may appear briefly in server logs for security and troubleshooting and isn't linked to your leaderboard entry.

There's no chat. We don't collect your real name, email, location, contacts, device identifiers or advertising ID.

What we don't do

  • No analytics SDKs and no accounts.
  • No selling of your data. The only third party is Google AdMob, as described above.

Children

The game isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game removes all data stored on your device. To remove your callsign and scores from the global leaderboard, email buidan.infotech@gmail.com with your callsign.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
