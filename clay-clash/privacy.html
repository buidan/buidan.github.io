Clay Clash: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Clay Clash ("the game") is made by Bui Dinh Dan. This policy explains what data the game handles. In short: no accounts and no analytics of our own; the game shows ads through Google AdMob, and online play sends gameplay data to our server.

Playing on your device

Your progress, settings, blob name, cosmetics and currencies are stored only on your device. Matches against bots don't send gameplay data anywhere (ads may still load if you're connected; see below).

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows optional rewarded ads (to get back on the table, double your loot or earn free Glaze Gems) and occasional full-screen ads between matches through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier, approximate location from your IP address, ad interactions and diagnostics, to show and measure ads. In the EU/UK you're asked for consent first (Google's consent form; change it any time under Profile → Privacy options). On iOS, ads are personalised only if you allow tracking. See how Google uses data from apps.

Online play and friend rooms

When you play online, the game connects over an encrypted connection (TLS) to our game server at play.swarmzone.click. The server receives:

  • Your blob name and look (colour, texture, prop, eyes), shown to other players in the match or room.
  • Your controls and in-game state, used only to run the match. They're held in memory and discarded when the match ends.
  • Your leaderboard entry: a random anonymous player id created on your device, your blob name, colour, trophy points and wins, shown on the public global leaderboard.
  • Your IP address, which is part of any internet connection. It may appear briefly in server logs for security and troubleshooting and isn't linked to your leaderboard entry.

There's no chat. We don't collect your real name, email, location, contacts or advertising ID.

Notifications

If you allow reminders, the game schedules local notifications on your device (daily chest, new Chaos event, a nudge after a few days away). They're created on the device; no push service or server is involved.

Purchases

If in-app purchases are offered, they're processed by Apple or Google. We never see your payment details.

What we don't do

  • No analytics SDKs and no accounts.
  • No selling of your data. The only third party is Google AdMob, as described above.

Children

The game isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game removes all data stored on your device. To remove your entry from the global leaderboard, email buidan.infotech@gmail.com with your blob name.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
