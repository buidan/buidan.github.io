Clay Clash: Support

Need help, found a bug or have an idea? Email buidan.infotech@gmail.com with your phone model and what happened. We usually answer within two days.

How do I play?

Drag anywhere on the left to roll your blob. Swipe quickly and let go for a Flick Boost. Hold ANCHOR to flatten and resist hits, tap EJECT MASS to fire clay wads. Shove everyone else off the table to win.

How do I play with friends?

Pick Sculpt-Off, tap CREATE ROOM and share the 4-letter code. Friends pick Sculpt-Off, enter the code and tap JOIN. The host presses START; bots fill any empty seats.

Online play won't connect

Check your internet connection, then make sure you have the latest version from the App Store or Google Play. You can always play against bots offline.

My progress

Progress is saved on your device. Deleting the app deletes your progress.
